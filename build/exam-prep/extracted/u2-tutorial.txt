12. Joel owns and manages a sports shop named ‘Sports Magic’ that uses the accrual
method of accounting. Joel has been having a few issues recently that he needs
your help with:

On 11 August 20XX the business sold a basketball for $50 on credit to a regular customer
named Mel. Since the business has not received the money yet, Joel decides not to record
this as a sale. Mel pays the amount owing on 5 September 20XX, to which Joel then records
the cash received from Mel as a cash sale.

Required:
Advise Joel on his recording of the sale transactions. (Hint: Think of the accrual
method of accounting)
