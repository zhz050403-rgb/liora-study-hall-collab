2. The 5 essential requirements of accounting reports to aid usefulness are noted
below. Match the term and meaning.

Term
Answer
Definition
Clarity
(understandability):

A.  Users must be able to identify similarities
and differences between the current
information and information from previous
years' reports or reports of different
entities.



Accuracy
(reliability):

B. To be useful, the information prepared by
accountants must also be timely i.e. reports
should be prepared in plenty of time for the
people requiring the information to make
decisions. If information is not available
when it is needed, it is of no use.



Consistency
(comparability):

C. The reports are presented in such a way
that reasonably-informed users can
comprehend their meaning, e.g. the use of
headings and sub-headings may help to
highlight important information in a Profit
and Loss Statement.



Timely

D. Reports should comply with the
requirements of accounting regulations and
accounting standards.



Compliance

E. The information should correspond with the
actual underlying transactions and events,
and is capable of independent verification
and is free from bias. The reports can be
used to confirm or correct expectations
about past events and assist in forming,
revising or confirming expectations about
the future.
