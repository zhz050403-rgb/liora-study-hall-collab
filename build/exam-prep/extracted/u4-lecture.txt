=== PAGE 4 ===
Journal Entries
4
• Definition: The General Journal is a chronological
record of all financial transactions of a business.
• Purpose: It serves as the initial book of entry where
transactions are first recorded before they are posted to
individual accounts in the General Ledger.

=== PAGE 5 ===
Journal Entries
5
• Date: When the transaction occurred.
• Account Titles: Accounts affected by the transaction.
• Debits and Credits: Amounts to be debited and credited
to the respective accounts.
• Narration: A brief explanation of the transaction.

=== PAGE 11 ===
Trade Discount
•
You buy/sell (trade) often with a business or maybe buy in bulk amounts.
•
It has an immediate effect as the business is giving/receiving a discount at
the time of sale/purchase.
•
The trade discount is included in the invoice.
•
This trade discount is not recorded in the accounting books or records.
•
This is also known as a Price Discount.
1

=== PAGE 13 ===
Settlement Discount
•
Discounts are usually received/allowed to encourage early payment/receipt of
cash to settle an outstanding account/debt early.
•
This is also known as a prompt payment discount.
3
Discount Received
•
The business receives a discount from
creditors/accounts payable for early
payment of outstanding debt/amount
owing.
•
This discount is recognised as Revenue
as the business receives a benefit.
•
Less cash is paid to settle the
transaction.
Discount Allowed
•
The business allows/gives a discount
to the debtors/accounts receivable for
their early payment of outstanding
debt/amount owing.
•
This discount is recognised as an
Expense as it is a cost to the
business.
•
Less cash is received to settle the
transaction.

• A customer (Account receivable) will not be paying the

• Usually, the amount owing will not be paid in future
• It also needs to be written off (taken out) from Account
Receivable as the debt will not be paid
• It is a cost to the business and therefore an Expense
