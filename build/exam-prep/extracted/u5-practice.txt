Lecture Exercise: Unit 5b
Balancing an account (T format)
Given below are the transactions of Barry’s Doctor Clinic.
Transactions:
1 February - Barry (the owner) contributed $10,000 cash to the business
4 February - The business provided treatment (Service Revenue) worth $2,500 to Vicky on credit.
Hint: “on credit” means Vicky will pay cash later.
8 February - The business received $2,500 cash from Vicky for the treatment provided on 4
February. Hint: Remember to decrease the amount Vicki owes from Accounts Receivable.
21 February - Paid 3 months rent in advance (ie rent is for March, April and May) for $3,000 cash.
28 February - Received $1000 cash from Angelina for future treatment. Hint: the service hasn’t
been provided yet.
