例 1（Tutorial Terms） Explain: Adjusting Entry, Depreciation Expense, Accumulated Depreciation, Unearned Revenue, Prepaid Expense, Accrued Expenses, Accrued Revenue.

Adjusting Entry：期末为使收入、费用记入正确期间而做的分录，不涉及现金。
Depreciation Expense：本期分摊的非流动资产成本，是费用。
Accumulated Depreciation：累计折旧，资产的抵减账户（contra asset），贷方余额，在资产负债表上从资产原值中扣除。
Unearned Revenue：预收收入，已收款、尚未提供商品或服务，是流动负债。
Prepaid Expense：预付费用，已付款、尚未受益，是流动资产。
Accrued Expenses：应计费用，已发生、未付款也未入账，期末记费用和应付。
Accrued Revenue：应计收入，已赚到、未收款也未入账，期末记应收和收入。

例 7（Tutorial Q2） Shell Company paid $12,000 on 1 January for a one-year insurance policy. Prepare the adjusting entry at year end 30 June.

1 月 1 日到 6 月 30 日已过 6 个月：12 000 × 6/12 = 6 000 转为费用，另 6 000 仍是预付保险（资产）。

General Journal 普通日记账
DateParticularsDr $Cr $
June 30Insurance Expense6,000
Prepaid Insurance6,000
(Insurance expired 1 January – 30 June)

例 8（Tutorial Q3） Jade Corporation received $10,000 in advance for IT services over the next five months, charged equally each month. Record the receipt and the adjusting entry at the end of the first month (Jan 31).

每月 10 000 ÷ 5 = 2 000。
注：题目没写收款日期，按 1 月 1 日处理。

General Journal 普通日记账
DateParticularsDr $Cr $
Jan 1Cash10,000
Unearned Revenue10,000
(Received in advance for 5 months of IT services)
Jan 31Unearned Revenue2,000
Service Revenue2,000
(IT services provided in January)

例 9（Tutorial Q4） On 1 June 20X1, ABC Enterprises purchased a one-year insurance policy for $9,000. At the end of June $750 had expired. Prepare the original entry on 1 June and the adjusting entry on 30 June.

9 000 ÷ 12 = 750，与题目一致。

例 13（Tutorial Q1） Kingsford Trading purchased equipment for $30,000 on 1 January, useful life 5 years, no salvage value. Calculate the annual depreciation and make the adjusting entry on 31 December.

(30 000 − 0) ÷ 5 = 6 000，1 月 1 日到 12 月 31 日正好一年。

General Journal 普通日记账
DateParticularsDr $Cr $
31 DecDepreciation Expense6,000
Accumulated Depreciation – Equipment6,000
(Depreciation for the year)

例 21（Tutorial Q5） Sunny Day Designs borrowed $50,000 on 1 June; loan and all interest due in 3 months; 6% p.a. Prepare the 1 June entry and the 30 June adjustment.

6 月份利息 50 000 × 6% × 1/12 = 250。

例 22（Tutorial Q7） Willy Wonka borrowed $100,000 on 1 June 2024 for 6 years at 6% p.a., interest payable every six months; accounts close 30 June. a) Prepare the 30 June adjustment. b) Prepare the entry on 30 November when interest is paid.

a) 6 月 1 个月：100 000 × 6% × 1/12 = 500。
b) 11 月 30 日付 6 个月利息 100 000 × 6% × 6/12 = 3 000：500 冲减应付利息，2 500（7–11 月）是新一年的费用。

General Journal 普通日记账
DateParticularsDr $Cr $
30 JuneInterest Expense500
Interest Payable500
(Accrued interest for June)
General Journal 普通日记账
DateParticularsDr $Cr $
30 NovInterest Payable500
Interest Expense2,500
Cash3,000
(Paid six months interest)

例 23（Tutorial Q8） Willow Wind closes its accounts on 31 October 2024. Not yet in the books: a. electricity bill $1,200 unrecorded, payable next month; b. insurance policy $9,600 for 1 year from 1 May 2024; c. services performed for $3,000 of revenue received in advance; d. salaries of $6,400 owed at 31 October; e. office equipment $86,400, life 5 years, no resale value; f. invoices for $8,000 of services performed not recorded; g. bank loan $50,000 at 10% p.a. from 1 June 2024. Required a) adjusting entries. Required b) Salaries of $10,000 were paid on 5 November for the last two weeks — record it.

a. 应计费用 1 200。
b. 5 月 1 日到 10 月 31 日 6 个月：9 600 × 6/12 = 4 800 已过期。
c. 预收转收入 3 000。
d. 应计工资 6 400。
e. 年折旧 86 400 ÷ 5 = 17 280。
f. 应计收入 8 000。
g. 6 月 1 日到 10 月 31 日 5 个月：50 000 × 10% × 5/12 = 2 083.33。
Required b) 付 10 000：6 400 冲减应付工资，3 600 是 11 月的费用。
注：b 题没说保险费付款时如何入账，按常规（付款时记 Prepaid Insurance）处理；e 题按满一年计提。

例 24（Tutorial Q9） Lois Lane Books closes on 31 December 2024. Not included: A insurance expires at $210 per month; B building cost $150,000, no salvage, life 30 years; C mortgage at 12% on a $100,000 loan from 1 May 2024; D a customer paid $5,000 in advance for a George Eliot collection, $3,000 provided by 31 December; E salaries of $500 unpaid; F a customer bought the Jane Austen collection on 30 December 2024 for $750, paying in 2 weeks. Required a) adjusting entries. Required b) The building was purchased on 1 January 2015; show the depreciation presentation in the balance sheet at 31 December 2024.

A 全年 12 × 210 = 2 520。
B 150 000 ÷ 30 = 5 000。
C 5 月 1 日到 12 月 31 日 8 个月：100 000 × 12% × 8/12 = 8 000。
D 预收转收入 3 000，余 2 000 仍是负债。
E 应计工资 500。
F 赊销 750（已卖出，收入属于 2024 年）。
Required b) 2015 年 1 月 1 日到 2024 年 12 月 31 日共 10 年，累计折旧 10 × 5 000 = 50 000，账面价值 100 000。
注：A 只给了每月金额，按全年（12 个月）计算；如果老师只要求某一个月，就是 210。
