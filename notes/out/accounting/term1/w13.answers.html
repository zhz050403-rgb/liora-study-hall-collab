例 1（Sample Exam MCQ） 1 Owner's equity from the accounting equation; 2 why Discount Allowed is debited; 3 receipt of cash from a debtor; 4 bad debts written off are debited to; 5 not an equivalent of inventory; 6 COGS with opening inventory $20,000, purchases $105,000, purchase returns $2,000, freight $3,000, closing inventory $28,000; 7 incorrect statement about the statement of cash flows; 8 convention that ensures equality of the accounting equation; 9 adjustments to accrue revenues; 10 a trial balance will not balance if.

1 A：A = L + OE + (R − E)，所以 OE = A − L − (R − E)。
2 D：Discount Allowed 是费用，费用增加记借方。
3 D：现金（资产）增加记借方，应收账款（资产）减少记贷方。
4 D：Bad debts 账户。
5 D：drawings 是提款，不是存货。
6 D：20 000 + 105 000 − 2 000 + 3 000 − 28 000 = 98 000。
7 C：现金流量表调节的是期初和期末现金余额，不是与银行对账单核对。
8 D：复式记账惯例。
9 B：应计收入使资产（应收）和收入都增加。
10 C：借 2 000、贷 200，借贷不等。A、B、D 两边金额都相等，试算表仍平衡。

例 2（Sample Exam Q11） For each transaction state where it is reported on the statement of cash flows and whether it is a cash inflow, outflow or no effect: (a) recorded depreciation expense on plant assets; (b) recorded and paid interest expense; (c) recorded cash proceeds from a sale of equipment; (e) paid a cash dividend to shareholders; (g) recorded cash sales; (h) recorded sales on account; (i) purchased inventory for cash; (j) purchased inventory on account.

(a) 不列示，没有现金影响（折旧不付现金）
(b) 经营活动，流出
(c) 投资活动，流入
(e) 筹资活动，流出
(g) 经营活动，流入
(h) 不列示，没有现金影响（赊销还没收到钱）
(i) 经营活动，流出
(j) 不列示，没有现金影响（赊购还没付钱）
注：样卷的 (d)、(f) 两行被涂黑，看不到内容，没有作答。

例 4（Sample Exam Q13） Wilson Trading paid Bailey Holdings $3,500 for goods purchased in the previous month. As Bailey Holdings' accountant (accrual method), explain and identify the 5 stages of the accounting cycle for this transaction, including the trial balance.

1 交易：收到客户 Wilson Trading 偿还的欠款 3 500（上月赊销时已确认收入，这次不是新收入）。
2 原始凭证：Bailey 开给 Wilson 的收据（receipt）副本，以及银行存款记录。
3 日记账：Dr Cash at Bank 3 500；Cr Accounts Receivable – Wilson Trading 3 500；摘要「Received payment from Wilson Trading for last month's credit sale」。
4 分类账：过账到 Cash at Bank 账户借方（摘要写 Accounts Receivable）和 Accounts Receivable 账户贷方（摘要写 Cash at Bank），月末结账。
5 试算平衡表：现金余额增加、应收账款余额减少，借贷两栏同时变化，合计仍相等。

例 5（Sample Exam Q14） Jim's Bookshop Ltd ($m): Cash 205 → 208; Accounts Receivable 12.5 → 102.8; Inventory 4.8 → 5.0; Buildings 6.2 → 6.2; Total assets 228.5 → 322 (2021 → 2022). 1 Describe the trend in total assets. 2 Suggest two major reasons. 3 Is the change a positive or negative indicator of financial health?

1 总资产从 228.5 增加到 322，增加 93.5（约 40.9%）。
2 几乎全部来自应收账款：从 12.5 增加到 102.8（+90.3）。原因可能是 ① 赊销大幅增加（卖得更多，但大多是赊账）；② 客户付款变慢，或放宽了信用条件。
3 喜忧参半。如果是销售增长带来的，是好事；但新增资产几乎都是还没收到的钱，现金只增加了 3，存在坏账和现金短缺的风险，要检查收账期是否变长。总体应视为需要警惕的信号。

例 9（Sample Exam Q18） Part A: Tsobo Limited cash transactions (year ending June 2021): payment of interest 15,000; cash sales 60,000; receipt of dividend revenue 14,000; payment of income tax 16,000; profit 38,000; payment of accounts payable for inventory 100,000; payment for land 74,000; collection of accounts receivable 190,000; payment of salaries and wages 68,000; depreciation expense 16,000; proceeds from sale of aircraft 812,000; purchase of equipment for cash 22,000; loss on sale of aircraft 3,000; payment of dividends 14,000; payment of operating expenses 20,000. Calculate net cash from investing activities. Part B: Tsobo Jeans ratios — current ratio 2:1 (2020), 1:1 (2021), industry 2.5:1; quick ratio 1.5:1, 1.5:1, industry 1:1. 1a discuss the current ratio trend; 1b explain the quick ratio trend; 1c two reasons for the current ratio trend given the quick ratio; 2 from the current assets and liabilities (Cash 45,000/44,800; A/R 41,000/40,000; Inventory 60,000/30,000; Prepaid rent 12,000/12,000; Bank loan 50,000/50,000; A/P 120,000/20,000 for 2021/2020) identify two reasons; 3 should JoJo's sell to Tsobo on credit?

Part A：投资活动 = 出售飞机 812 000 − 买设备 22 000 − 买土地 74 000 + 收到股利 14 000 = 730 000（净流入）。利润、折旧、出售损失都不是现金流；利息、税、工资、供应商、营业费用属于经营活动；支付股利属于筹资活动。
1a 流动比率从 2:1 降到 1:1，减半，且远低于行业平均 2.5:1：流动资产刚好够还流动负债，短期偿债能力明显变差。
1b 速动比率保持 1.5:1，高于行业平均 1:1：扣除存货和预付后的资产对短期负债的覆盖没有变差。
1c 速动比率不变而流动比率下降，说明变化发生在存货和流动负债上：① 赊购了大量存货，存货和应付账款都增加；② 流动负债（应付账款）大幅增加。
2 应付账款从 20 000 增加到 120 000（+100 000）；存货从 30 000 增加到 60 000（翻倍）。用这些数字算，流动比率从 126 800 ÷ 70 000 = 1.81 降到 158 000 ÷ 170 000 = 0.93。
3 不建议赊销，或只给很短的信用期和较低额度：流动比率已低于 1，应付账款一年增加了 5 倍，现金几乎没增加，Tsobo 可能付不起新的欠款；可以要求现金交易或预付款。
注：讲义的现金流分类表把收到的股利列为投资活动（也可列为经营），这里按投资活动；若列为经营，投资活动净额为 716 000。第 2 问给出的明细算出的比率（1.81 和 0.93，速动比率 1.21 和 0.51）与表中的 2:1、1:1、1.5:1 不完全一致，答题时以各问给出的数据为准。
