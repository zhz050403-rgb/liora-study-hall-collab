UNIT 1 | Introduction to Economics

依据：2026 U1 Lecture 1.1–1.2 / Tutorial；以及既有“unit0–1 NOTE revised complete with market systems”文档。

核心知识笔记

Economics: The study of how people and societies allocate scarce resources to satisfy unlimited wants.

经济学研究个人与社会如何在资源有限的情况下做出选择、分配资源并满足欲望。

Scarcity: The condition where unlimited wants exceed available resources.

稀缺性来自无限欲望与有限资源的矛盾。稀缺性导致必须取舍。

Economy: A system in which resources are used to produce and distribute goods and services.

经济是生产、分配、交换和消费的活动体系。

Microeconomics: The study of individual consumers, firms and markets.

微观经济学关注单个消费者、企业以及具体市场。

Macroeconomics: The study of the economy as a whole, including inflation, unemployment and growth.

宏观经济学关注通胀、失业与经济增长等总量问题。

Four Factors of Production

FactorDefinition (easy English)ExamplesReward / 典型回报
LandNatural resources used in productionLand, minerals, forestsRent
LabourHuman physical and mental effortWorkers, teachers, chefsWages
CapitalHuman-made productive resourcesMachines, factories, toolsReturn on capital / Interest*
EnterpriseOrganising resources and taking business risksFounder, entrepreneurProfit

* “资本报酬”在某些旧课件表述存在术语差异；课堂答题需与本班教师表述保持一致。消费品手机若用于日常消费通常不属于生产资本，若作为企业生产用设备则可能构成资本投入。

典型考点

  • Why must choices be made? → Resources are scarce, while wants are unlimited.
  • Difference between goods and services → Goods are tangible; services are intangible activities.
  • Entrepreneur vs labour → Enterprise organises and bears risk; labour provides human work/skills.

U1 题目与参考答案

U1-Q1. Define scarcity and explain why it leads to choice.

出处/性质：官方 U1 Lecture 概念 + Tutorial Terms，整理题

参考答案：Scarcity means available resources cannot satisfy all wants. Individuals, firms and governments must choose between competing uses of limited resources.

U1-Q2. Classify: fishing grounds, cash register, university lecturer and factory robot into Land / Labour / Capital.

出处/性质：2026 U1 Tutorial Q3 选项摘选

参考答案：Fishing grounds = Land; cash register = Capital; university lecturer = Labour; robot in a factory = Capital.

U1-Q3. What is the difference between microeconomics and macroeconomics?

出处/性质：2026 U1 Lecture/Tutorial 重点，整理题

参考答案：Microeconomics studies individuals, firms and particular markets. Macroeconomics studies economy-wide outcomes such as inflation, unemployment and economic growth.

U1-Q4. Why may one person earn more than another?

出处/性质：2026 U1 Tutorial Q5 题意整理

参考答案：Differences in education, skills, productivity, scarcity of skills, labour demand and experience can affect wages and other incomes.

UNIT 2 | Production and Technology

依据：2026 U2 Lecture 2.1–2.2 / Tutorial，以及既有“unit2 NOTE production and technology”文档。

核心知识笔记

Three economic questions: What to produce? How to produce? For whom to produce?

资源稀缺迫使经济体决定生产什么、如何生产以及分配给谁。

Opportunity cost: The value of the next best alternative forgone when making a choice.

机会成本是作出选择时放弃的最佳替代方案的价值。

PPC / PPF: A curve showing the maximum attainable combinations of two goods with given resources and technology.

生产可能性曲线显示在既定资源、技术和有效利用条件下，两种商品的最大可实现组合。

Marginal analysis: Comparing the additional benefits and additional costs of an action.

边际分析比较某一决定带来的额外收益与额外成本。

Opportunity Cost of additional X = Quantity of Y forgone ÷ Additional quantity of X

Decision rule (simple): choose an additional unit when Marginal Benefit ≥ Marginal Cost

PPC 的位置与变化

Position / movementInterpretation
On the PPCProductively efficient: existing resources fully and efficiently used
Inside PPCAttainable but productively inefficient / unemployment
Outside PPCUnattainable with current resources and technology
Movement along PPCReallocation of resources; opportunity cost / trade-off
Outward shiftGrowth of resources, capital accumulation or technological improvement
Inward shiftLoss of resources or destruction of productive capacity

Source: official Unit 2 Lecture/Tutorial PPC example; values shown in thousands.

U2 官方 PPC 实例计算

官方 Tutorial Q3：A(Chocolate=100k, Machines=0k); B(90k,2.5k); C(70k,5k); D(0k,7.5k)。从 B→C，多生产 2.5k 台机器，少生产 20k 块巧克力。

OC of 2,500 machines = 20,000 chocolate bars; OC per machine = 20,000 ÷ 2,500 = 8 bars

考试警示 / EXAM CHECK Law of Increasing Opportunity Costs：沿 PPC 持续增加某一商品产量，生产每单位该商品所放弃的另一商品数量通常上升；原因是资源对不同用途的适用性不同。

U2 题目与参考答案

U2-Q1. What does “there is no such thing as a free lunch” mean?

出处/性质：2026 U2 Tutorial Q1 题意整理

参考答案：Every choice uses scarce resources and involves an opportunity cost, even when no direct monetary payment is required.

U2-Q2. Using the official PPC: what is sacrificed when machines increase from 2,500 to 5,000?

出处/性质：2026 U2 Tutorial Q3(b)

参考答案：Chocolate output falls from 90,000 to 70,000. Opportunity cost is 20,000 chocolate bars in total, or 8 bars per additional machine.

U2-Q3. If chocolate output rises from 90,000 to 100,000, what is the opportunity cost?

出处/性质：2026 U2 Tutorial Q3(c)

参考答案：The economy moves from B to A and sacrifices 2,500 machines.

U2-Q4. Why is a PPC bowed outward rather than a straight line?

出处/性质：2026 U2 Tutorial Q3(d)

参考答案：Resources are not equally well suited to producing both goods. As more resources are transferred, the opportunity cost of additional output increases.

U2-Q5. Explain a PPC outward shift versus movement along the PPC.

出处/性质：2026 U2 Lecture/Tutorial Q4 整理

参考答案：An outward shift represents higher productive capacity due to more resources or technology. Movement along an unchanged PPC represents a different allocation of existing resources.

UNIT 3 | Demand and Supply

依据：2026 U3 Lecture / Tutorial；较早版本的逐题聊天记录未完整取得，题库以官方 Tutorial 可核实题意为基础。

核心知识笔记

Law of Demand: Other things equal, quantity demanded falls when price rises and rises when price falls.

需求定律：其他条件不变，价格升高时需求量下降。

Law of Supply: Other things equal, quantity supplied rises when price rises.

供给定律：其他条件不变，价格升高时供给量上升。

Change in quantity demanded: A movement along the demand curve caused by a change in the good’s own price.

自身价格变化引起沿原需求曲线移动。

Change in demand: A shift of the entire demand curve caused by non-price determinants.

收入、偏好、替代品价格等非价格因素变化引起整条需求曲线移动。

Determinants / 方向判断

ScenarioDemand / Supply change
Income ↑ (normal good)Demand → right
Income ↑ (inferior good)Demand → left
Price of a substitute ↑Demand for this good → right
Price of a complement ↑Demand for this good → left
Input costs ↑Supply → left
Technology improvementSupply → right
Own price ↑Movement along same D/S curve; no curve shift

U4 Lecture 原始均衡表：P=$3 时 Qd=Qs=12，兼用于 U3 均衡概念巩固。

图形分析答题模板

Cause → Which curve shifts (D or S) → Direction → New equilibrium price → New equilibrium quantity. 两条曲线同时移动时，如未给幅度，不可随意确定某个变量的最终方向。

U3 题目与参考答案

U3-Q1. Explain the Law of Demand and why the demand curve usually slopes downward.

出处/性质：2026 U3 Tutorial Q1 题意整理

参考答案：Other things equal, a higher price reduces quantity demanded. Consumers may switch to substitutes and their purchasing power is reduced.

U3-Q2. A successful advertising campaign increases consumers’ preference for coffee. What happens to demand?

出处/性质：2026 U3 Tutorial Q3(b)

参考答案：Demand for coffee increases; the demand curve shifts to the right.

U3-Q3. Tea is a substitute for coffee. The price of tea falls. What happens to coffee demand?

出处/性质：2026 U3 Tutorial Q3(c)

参考答案：Some consumers switch from coffee to cheaper tea; demand for coffee shifts left.

U3-Q4. The price of hotel rooms rises because of a hotel tax. Does the original hotel demand curve shift?

出处/性质：2026 U3 Tutorial Q4(a)

参考答案：A change in the hotel room’s own price causes a movement along its existing demand curve (quantity demanded falls), other things equal.

U3-Q5. Coffee producers benefit from cheaper pesticides. Predict the supply curve shift.

出处/性质：2026 U3 Tutorial Q7(a)

参考答案：Production costs decrease, so supply increases and the supply curve shifts right.

UNIT 4 | Market Successes and Failures

依据：2026 U4 Lecture 4.1–4.2 / Tutorial，及此前生成的 Unit 4 Markets in Action Notes。

Market Equilibrium / Price Controls

Market equilibrium: The price and quantity at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

市场均衡：需求量与供给量相等，交点为 E，纵轴是价格，横轴是数量。

Shortage: Quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied at the current price.

短缺：Qd > Qs，价格有上行压力。

Surplus: Quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded at the current price.

过剩：Qs > Qd，价格有下行压力。

Price ceiling: A legal maximum price. A binding ceiling below equilibrium causes a shortage.

约束性最高限价低于均衡价时出现短缺，如租金管制。

Price floor: A legal minimum price. A binding floor above equilibrium causes a surplus.

约束性最低限价高于均衡价时出现过剩。最低工资情形常用劳动力市场说明。

Market Failure / Public Goods

Market failure: A situation where the market fails to allocate resources efficiently.

市场失灵：价格机制未能实现有效资源配置。

Public goods: Goods that are non-rival and non-excludable.

公共物品：非竞争性、非排他性，可能存在搭便车问题。

Externality: A cost or benefit imposed on a third party not reflected in a market transaction.

外部性：市场交易影响未参与交易的第三方，影响未充分反映在私人价格中。

Negative / Positive Externality

Negative production externality: MSC = MPC + MEC → MSC > MPC → Qmarket > Qsocial

Positive consumption externality: MSB = MPB + MEB → MSB > MPB → Qmarket < Qsocial

负生产外部性示意：MSC 在 MPC 上方，市场产量 Qm 高于社会最优 Q*（非原始课件坐标值）。

考试警示 / EXAM CHECK 用户 U4 的历史独立复测：化工厂污染影响附近居民 → MPC < MSC → 市场过度生产。此前这两个方向的概念已做过 2/2 针对性练习；外部性完整图形当时未做独立验证。

U4 题目与参考答案

U4-Q1. Official lecture: at P=$2, Qd=16 and Qs=8. Calculate the shortage.

出处/性质：2026 U4 Lecture 4.1 原始数据

参考答案：Shortage = 16 − 8 = 8 units. Competition among buyers puts upward pressure on price.

U4-Q2. Define a binding price ceiling and explain its likely effect.

出处/性质：2026 U4 Lecture/Tutorial 整理题

参考答案：A binding price ceiling is a legal maximum below equilibrium. At that price quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied, creating a shortage.

U4-Q3. Why may public goods be underprovided by private firms?

出处/性质：2026 U4 Tutorial Q3 题意整理

参考答案：Public goods are non-rival and non-excludable. Some consumers can benefit without paying, which weakens private firms’ incentive to supply them.

U4-Q4. A chemical factory pollutes nearby residents. Explain MSC versus MPC and market output.

出处/性质：历史 U4 独立测试/错题复测，按用户实际回答重述

参考答案：The factory does not bear all external costs. MSC exceeds MPC; the market tends to produce more than the socially optimal quantity.

历史作答/纠错：用户曾能正确解释化工厂污染、第三方受损、MSC > MPC、overproduction。

U4-Q5. A positive externality in education is not reflected in private demand. What is the result?

出处/性质：2026 U4 Tutorial Q5 题意整理

参考答案：MSB exceeds MPB; the market tends to underconsume education relative to the socially optimal quantity. A suitable subsidy may help close the gap.

UNIT 5 | Pricing and Income of the Firm and Its Customers

依据：2026 U5 Lecture 5.1–5.2 / Tutorial；本窗 6 题独立练习与 2 题定向复测完整保留。

Elasticities: definitions and formulas

TermEnglish definitionFormula / classification
PEDResponsiveness of Q demanded to own price%ΔQd / %ΔP; sign usually negative; classify by |PED|
YEDResponsiveness of Q demanded to income%ΔQd / %ΔIncome; + normal / − inferior
XEDResponsiveness of demand for A to price of B%ΔQ_A / %ΔP_B; + substitutes / − complements
PESResponsiveness of Q supplied to price%ΔQs / %ΔP; >1 elastic / <1 inelastic

Base-point % change = (New − Old) / Old × 100%

Midpoint % change = (New − Old) / [(New + Old)/2] × 100%

考试警示 / EXAM CHECK 基期法和中点法结果可能不同；PED 在经济学上常为负。官方课件常以绝对值表述 Elastic / Inelastic；XED 和 YED 必须保留符号以判断关系或商品类型。

PED and Total Revenue

ElasticityPrice increasesPrice decreases
Elastic (|PED| > 1)TR decreasesTR increases
Inelastic (|PED| < 1)TR increasesTR decreases
Unit elastic (|PED| = 1)TR unchanged (local approximation)TR unchanged (local approximation)

TR = Price × Quantity | AR = TR / Q | MR = ΔTR / ΔQ

课上曾用线性需求 P=10−Q 说明 TR 的最大值及 MR 与 TR 的关系（模型例题）。

Elasticity common determinants

  • PED：Substitutes availability, necessity vs luxury, share of income, time to adjust.
  • PES：Time period, spare production capacity, stocks, resource mobility, producer expectations.

U5｜第一轮六道独立测试（用户已答）

U5-P1. Price $7→$6; Qd 25→35. Calculate PED using the MIDPOINT formula and TR before/after.

出处/性质：2026 U5 Tutorial Q1 数据 + 本窗独立测试

U5-O1. Fashion garment: P=$70→$50, Q=7000→7800. Find PED (base-point) and receipts.

UNIT 6 | Equilibria in Production

依据：2026 U6 Lecture 6.1–6.2 / Tutorial；本窗包含 4 题、AVC复测、6题综合、3题复测、2题复测、最终确认。

Costs / Profit / Time period

Explicit cost: Direct monetary payment for production resources.

显性成本包括工资、租金、材料费等真实货币支出。

Implicit cost: Opportunity cost of resources owned and used by the firm.

隐性成本包括企业主放弃的替代工资、资金使用的机会成本等。

Accounting Profit = TR − Explicit Costs

Economic Profit = TR − Explicit Costs − Implicit Costs

Short run: A production period with at least one fixed input. Long run: All inputs can be varied.

短期至少一种生产要素固定；长期可调整全部投入，时间长度不是统一的日历天数。

Normal profit: Minimum return needed to keep a firm in its current activity; economic profit equals zero.

正常利润已经覆盖经济意义上的机会成本，经济利润为零。

TP, MP, AP and the law of diminishing returns

MP = ΔTP / ΔLabour | AP = TP / Labour

当追加劳动使 MP 增加时，TP 增长加快。MP 下降但保持正值时，TP 仍增长但增速放缓。MP 变负则 TP 直接下降。

U6 Tutorial 成本表所绘的离散曲线：AFC 持续下降，AVC、ATC 近似 U 形；MC 于 AVC/ATC 低点附近交会。

Why is ATC U-shaped? AFC decreases as fixed costs are spread, and specialisation can initially reduce AVC. Diminishing returns eventually push AVC and ATC up.

ATC 初期因分摊固定成本及专业分工而下降；后期边际报酬递减令 AVC 上升并最终推高 ATC。

If MC < ATC → ATC falls; if MC > ATC → ATC rises (marginal-average principle).

Long-run costs, scale and profit

LRATC: Minimum attainable average cost for each output level when all inputs are variable.

长期平均成本曲线反映企业可改变全部生产要素时各产量对应的最低平均成本。

Internal economies: Cost advantages from a firm’s own expansion. External economies: Cost advantages caused by industry growth.

内部规模经济来源于企业自身扩大规模；外部规模经济来源于整个行业环境的发展。

TypeExampleCost implication
Internal economiesFirm gets bulk-buying discountsLRATC may fall as own scale rises
Internal diseconomiesOvergrown firm faces communication problemsLRATC may rise
External economiesIndustry attracts specialised suppliersFirm-level LRATC may shift down
External diseconomiesIndustry-wide congestion raises costsFirm-level LRATC may shift up

Profit = TR − TC; firm selects output where profit is greatest; standard interior condition MR = MC.

UNIT 7 | Market Structures: Perfect Competition

重要：2026 O2 Course Outline 已核实 U7 标题与 Week 9；当前未取得本班 U7 Lecture/Tutorial 原件。以下为此前会话已讲授的标准微观经济学模型预习。

Perfect Competition: definitions and assumptions (preliminary)

Perfect competition: A market structure with many buyers and sellers, homogeneous products, free entry and exit, and price-taking firms.

完全竞争市场包含大量买卖者、标准化产品、进入退出相对自由、单个企业接受市场价格等模型假设。

Price taker: A firm that accepts the prevailing market price because its own output cannot materially influence that price.

价格接受者无法通过自身少量生产决策影响行业价格。

For a price-taking firm: P = AR = MR = Dfirm

通用预习示意：左侧 Market D/S 决定价格；右侧单个企业需求为水平线。并非 2026 O2 U7 官方讲义截图。

Profit / loss and shutdown (preliminary)

TR = P×Q; Profit = (P − ATC)×Q at the specified output

Interior profit-maximising condition: MR = MC; price taker: P = MR = MC

Price relative to costStandard short-run implication
P > ATCPositive economic profit at the chosen quantity
P = ATCZero economic profit / normal profit
AVC ≤ P < ATCEconomic loss; may still produce to cover part of fixed costs
P < min AVCShut down in short run (standard model)

Long-run adjustment (preliminary)

  • If current firms make economic profits → entry → industry supply rightward → market P declines → excess profits eliminated.
  • If current firms make losses → exit → industry supply leftward → market P rises → losses eliminated.

Standard long-run equilibrium: P = MR = MC = min ATC; Economic profit = 0

出处/性质：本窗先前已布置 / 尚无用户作答

参考答案：Shut down under the standard rule because market price is below minimum average variable cost.

U7-P4. Firms earn economic profits. Explain the long-run adjustment.

参考答案：Entry increases number of firms and market supply, lowering market price and economic profits until normal profit is restored under the standard assumptions.
